How it works

Your readiness sets
how you train today.

Attune reads your readiness each day from your check in and your wearables, then sets your training intensity to match. Your hormonal phase adds context, so the plan understands your body instead of just following a calendar.

🌑
Menstrual
DAYS 1 TO 5
Estrogen and progesterone sit at their lowest. Energy and recovery often dip here, which your readiness score picks up so the plan eases when it should.
🌒
Follicular
DAYS 6 TO 13
Estrogen climbs and many women feel stronger and more recovered. When your readiness rises with it, Attune leans into harder work.
🌕
Ovulatory
DAYS 14 TO 17
Estrogen peaks and strength can feel highest. Readiness still has the final say day to day, so the plan never assumes how you feel.
🌖
Luteal
DAYS 18 TO 28
Progesterone rises and sleep and recovery can suffer. Readiness usually guides a steadier approach, with more fuel to match the higher demand.

Yes. Attune adapts your workouts and nutrition when you have PCOS, and it is also built for perimenopause, hypothyroidism, endometriosis, and women using GLP-1 medication.
How is Attune different from a normal workout app?
Attune sets your training intensity from your daily readiness rather than a fixed plan. It reads your morning check in, your Oura ring, and Apple Health, and factors in your hormones so the workout fits how your body actually feels today.
Does Attune work for perimenopause and GLP-1 medication?
Yes. Perimenopause and GLP-1 are built in conditions. Attune adjusts your strength training and your protein and energy targets to support you through them.
Does Attune connect with Oura and Apple Health?
Yes. Attune syncs with the Oura ring and Apple Health to read your sleep, recovery, and activity, which all feed your daily readiness score.
