Questions

Common questions.

Is Attune a fitness app for PCOS?

Yes. Attune adapts your workouts, meals and lab panel when you have PCOS, and it is also built for thyroid conditions, perimenopause, endometriosis and women using GLP-1 medication.

How is Attune different from a normal workout app?

Attune sets your training from your daily readiness rather than a fixed plan. It reads your morning check in, your Oura ring, WHOOP and Apple Health, and uses your condition and hormones as context, so the workout fits how your body actually feels today.

Does Attune work for perimenopause and GLP-1 medication?

Yes. Perimenopause and GLP-1 are built in conditions. Attune adjusts your strength training and your protein and energy targets to support you through them.

Does Attune connect with Oura and Apple Health?

Yes. Attune syncs with the Oura ring, WHOOP and Apple Health to read your sleep, recovery and activity, which all feed your daily readiness.
